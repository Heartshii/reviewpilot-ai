# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want ReviewPilot AI to stay respectful, collaborative, and safe for contributors.

Please:

- communicate clearly and kindly
- assume good intent
- welcome feedback and questions
- focus criticism on the work, not the person

## Unacceptable behavior

- harassment or intimidation
- discriminatory or abusive language
- personal attacks
- repeated disruption after clear feedback

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content, reject contributions, or restrict participation when conduct harms the project or its contributors.
